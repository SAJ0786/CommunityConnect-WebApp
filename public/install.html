Community Connect Australia

Keep your community close

Open Community Connect in your browser, add the web app to your device, or choose an existing store app.

Add the web app to your device

Start at communityconnect.siza.info. Browser menus vary by version; installation is optional.

iPhone or iPad

Open the site in Safari. Open Share (sometimes inside the page menu), choose Add to Home Screen, then Add. If shown, leave Open as Web App enabled.

Android

In Chrome, open the menu beside the address bar. Choose Install and create shortcut, then Install. Some versions show Add to Home screen. Follow the device prompts.

Computer

In Chrome, use the install icon when shown, or the menu's Cast, save, and share → Install page as app option. Other browsers may offer their own install command.

Already installed? Keep your existing icon. Updates are downloaded when you are connected; your browser may ask you to review a changed app name. You do not need to clear your browser data to update.

Previously visited screens may be available offline. Fresh listings, sign-in, messages, submissions and live streams need an internet connection.

What The App Includes

Explore community events and public business information. Available account features depend on the version you are using.

Business DirectoryFind businesses by city and service, read public details and explore current offers.
Events and Directions Browse community programs, view eligible addresses and open directions from event cards.
Calendar and Reminders Sync supported events to your calendar and receive reminder emails when enabled.
Live Streams Watch available public streams and manage authorised public or private event streams.
